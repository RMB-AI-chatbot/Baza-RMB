Management

RMB Management is assigned to regulate and manage Rwanda's extractive industry comprising of mineral, quarry, and upstream petroleum sectors as well as monitoring geohazards to guide urban planning and infrastructure development, mitigating economic losses and environmental impacts.

Senior Management

Alice UWASE

Chief Executive Officer

Ms. Alice UWASE was appointed Chief Executive Officer of the Rwanda Mines, Petroleum and Gas Board (RMB) by His Excellency President Paul Kagame on 18 July 2025. Prior to this role, Alice served as the Deputy Chief Executive Officer at RMB from 14 June 2024. She previously was the Head of the Mining, Petroleum, and Gas Exploration Department at RMB. Before joining RMB, Alice was the Gold Mining and Trading Project Manager at Ngali Mining, a subsidiary of NGALI Holdings. Alice brings extensive leadership experience in the mining, oil and gas sectors and is fluent in English, French, and Kinyarwanda.

Barbara AKALIZA

Chief Geo-Hazard Officer

Barbara AKALIZA

Chief Geo-Hazard Officer

Ms Barbara is the Chief Geo-Hazard Officer at the Rwanda Mines, Petroleum and Gas Board (RMB), where she leads national efforts in geohazard risk assessment, early warning systems, and disaster resilience. Her office coordinates the monitoring and mitigation of geological hazards such as landslides, earthquakes, and volcanic activity, contributing to climate adaptation and sustainable land use.

Barbara previously held leadership roles including Executive Manager for ABridge in Benin and Togo, Business Development Consultant for Right Seat in Rwanda and Board Member for Ngororero Mining Company. Her earlier technical experience includes working for NRD Rwanda and BRGM in France.

Barbara holds a Master’s degree in Geology and Environmental Engineering from Université Paris Sud XI and a Bachelor’s degree in Geology from Université Blaise Pascal. She is fluent in Kinyarwanda, French, and English.

Jean d’Amour UWIDUHAYE

Head of Department of Mines, Petroleum and Gas Exploration

Jean d’Amour UWIDUHAYE

Head of Department of Mines, Petroleum and Gas Exploration

Mr. UWIDUHAYE Jean d’Amour was appointed Head of the Department of Mines, Petroleum and Gas Exploration at the Rwanda Mines, Petroleum and Gas Board (RMB) by Cabinet on March 4, 2026.

Prior to his appointment, Mr. UWIDUHAYE served as a Geophysics Specialist in the Mining Exploration Division at RMB, a role he held from 2019. Before joining RMB, Mr. UWIDUHAYE worked as a Geothermal Specialist on the Geothermal Project at Energy Development Corporation Limited (EDCL), where he served from 2014 after joining from EWASA Ltd. 

Mr. UWIDUHAYE holds a Master of Engineering in Earth Resources Engineering from Kyushu University (Kyudai) in Fukuoka, Japan; and a Bachelor of Science in Physics from the National University of Rwanda. He is an active researcher in the geosciences, with particular interest in geophysical applications for natural resources prospecting. He is fluent in Kinyarwanda, English, and French.
 

Patrick NTALINDWA

Advisor to the Chief Executive Officer

Patrick NTALINDWA

Advisor to the Chief Executive Officer

Patrick is the Advisor to the Chief Executive Officer of Rwanda Mines, Petroleum and Gas Board (RMB) since his appointment by the Cabinet on January 30, 2023.

Prior to his appointment, Patrick was working for a World Bank Funded Project under LODA- Single Project Implementation Unit (SPIU) known as Rwanda Urban Development Project (RUDP) as a Project Monitoring and Evaluation Specialist from November 2019 up to January 2023.

Before joining the World Bank Project, he worked for Rwanda’s Ministry of Finance and Economic Planning (MINECOFIN) as the National Development Planner and Research Analyst from January 2013 to November 2019.

With the current role as Advisor to the CEO-RMB, Patrick’s responsibilities include but are not limited to: stakeholder engagement and profiling by conducting comprehensive profiling of mining companies to better understand their operational structures, production capacities, and specific business needs; serving as the primary point of contact for mining operators, undertaking high-level consultations to identify operational challenges and provide tailored solutions; coordinating the internal technical team and the CEO’s Office to ensure complex issues are addressed with accurate, data-driven expertise; coordination of licensing application assessment by managing the end-to-end review process for mining applications, ensuring all submissions met regulatory standards and technical requirements before license issuance; conducting on-site inspections to verify that mining operations comply with safety, environmental, and operational regulations; enforcement of industry compliance by monitoring mining activities and providing detailed assessment reports; Acting as a bridge between the CEO and key internal and external stakeholders, including government entities, international partners, and private sector investors; analyzing trends, risks, and opportunities in the mining, petroleum and gas sectors to inform the CEO's executive decisions; and leading the preparation of high-quality policy briefs, memos, presentations, and technical reports for the CEO’s office.

Patrick is a Certified Project Management Professional (PMP)® holding a Level-7 Certificate of Economic Analysis for Public Policy from King’s College London, a Diploma of Leadership and Management Styles from Alison Irish Institute, a Master of Business Administration in Project Management from the University of Kigali, as well as a Bachelor of Science in Economics from the National University of Rwanda. He is fluent in English, French and Kinyarwanda.

Alice UMUHORAKEYE

Mining Extraction & Inspection Division Manager.

Alice UMUHORAKEYE

Mining Extraction & Inspection Division Manager.

Ms. Alice Umuhorakeye currently serves as the Mining Extraction and Inspection Division Manager at the Rwanda Mines, Petroleum and Gas Board (RMB), where she oversees activities related to sustainable mineral resource management and regulatory compliance. She was appointed to this role by Cabinet on March 4, 2026.

Alice is an environmental professional with over 10 years of experience in environmental protection, environmental impact assessment (EIA), natural resources management, and climate change adaptation and mitigation.
Prior to joining RMB, she worked as a Technical Advisor at GIZ Rwanda from 2024 to 2026. In this role, she was responsible for coordinating the planning, implementation, and operationalization of Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) in alignment with national environmental policies and the Ministry of Environment’s guidance. She also ensured oversight of the NDC Financing Agreement between GIZ and the Ministry of Environment.

Previously, Alice served as a Water Quality Specialist at the Rwanda Water Resources Board from 2021 to 2024, where she contributed to monitoring and improving water quality and supported national water resources management strategies.

From 2015 to 2021, she worked as an Environmental Protection Specialist with the City of Kigali, focusing on environmental regulation, EIA processes, and urban environmental management.

Alice holds a master’s degree in environmental economics and Natural Resources Management and a Bachelor’s degree in Environmental Chemistry. Her professional work centers on promoting sustainable environmental practices, strengthening environmental governance, and supporting climate resilience initiatives. She is fluent in Kinyarwanda, English, and French.

Catherine KANTENGWA

Mining Cadaster & Digital Information Division Manager

Catherine KANTENGWA

Mining Cadaster & Digital Information Division Manager

Ms. Catherine KANTENGWA was appointed Division Manager of Mining cadaster and digital information at the Rwanda Mines, Petroleum and Gas Board (RMB) by Cabinet on March 4, 2026. 

Prior to her current appointment, Ms. Catherine served as Advisor to the Deputy Chief Executive Officer at RMB from 2018, where she provided technical and policy guidance on mining governance and institutional development. Her career foundation was built in the private sector, where she spent seven years as a Quality Control Analyst at Horizon SOPYRWA, honing her expertise in applied chemistry and industrial standards.

Catherine holds a Master’s Degree in Environmental Studies from the Open University of Tanzania and a Bachelor’s Degree in Applied Chemistry from Kigali Institute of Science and Technology (KIST). She further enriched her professional portfolio with a specialized certificate in Sustainable Development Impact Evaluation from Wageningen University in the Netherlands, underscoring her commitment to evidence-based policy and sustainable resource management. She is proficient in Kinyarwanda, English and French.
 

Ephrem MANIRAGABA

Mineral Exploration Division Manager

Ephrem MANIRAGABA

Mineral Exploration Division Manager


Mr. MANIRAGABA Ephrem was appointed Mining Exploration Division Manager at the Rwanda Mines, Petroleum and Gas Board (RMB) by Cabinet on March 4, 2026.

Prior to his appointment, Mr. Maniragaba served as a Geology Specialist at RMB from May 2025, having previously worked as a Geology Officer at the same institution from December 2022 to April 2025. Before joining RMB, he worked as a Mining Geologist at GAMICO Mining Ltd and as an Exploration Geologist with the China Geological Survey.

Mr. Maniragaba brings extensive experience in mineral exploration and geological research. He has led lithium exploration initiatives, supervised drilling campaigns, and coordinated multidisciplinary exploration teams. He is proficient in GIS and other geological software. He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Applied Geology from the University of Rwanda and is fluent in Kinyarwanda and English.
