MCIS Portal

Mineral Certification Information (MCIS) is used when applying for either Mineral Export Certificates or ICGLR Mineral Export Certificates. Companies allowed to export minerals have to request services related to mineral certification online.

Read more

GIMCS External Online Portal

Geological Information and Mining Cadaster System (GIMCS) External Online Portal is used by applicants requesting services related to applications for mining and quarry licenses, recommendations for blasting materials, and mineral sample permits.

Read more

MCIS - Mobile App

MCIS Mobile Application is used to verify the printed mineral certificates based on the information which is already in the Mineral Certification System (MCIS). MCIS Mobile App prevents the use of fake certificates.

Read more

RMB Cloud

RMB Data Cloud and Collaboration Platform (RMB Cloud) is used when sharing large files instead of using the emails which has size limitation. The RMB Cloud is also used to conduct simple surveys online.

Read more

GIMCS Internal Online Portal

Geological Information and Mining Cadaster System (GIMCS) Internal Online Portal is used by RMB and district staff to process applications for mining and quarry licenses, recommendations for blasting materials, and mineral sample permits.

Read more

GSAS

Government Smart Administration System (GSAS) or simply Smartadmin is used by RMB staff for the internal processing of documents that are to be approved or shared with the concerned staff.

Read more

RMB Webmail

The RMB Webmail is online accessed by RMB staff to ease internal communications with the usage of emails. It is also used to communicate to external users who do not have RMB official emails.

Read more

IPPIS / SmartHR

ntegrated Personel and Payroll Information System (IPPIS) or simply SmartHR is used to manage and process the evaluation of performance contracts, recruitment and payroll.
