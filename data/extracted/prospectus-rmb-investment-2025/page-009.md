# RWANDA'S MINERAL PORTFOLIO

| Minerals | Global standing | Key uses |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TANTALUM** | World's third - largest producer of tantalum, supplying a substantial portion of global demand for this electronic component. Home to Africa's only tantalum and niobium refinery | • Manufacturing electronic capacitors for devices like smartphones and laptops,<br>• Creating high-strength superalloys for aerospace and chemical processing equipment.<br>• Valuable for medical implants, surgical tools, and components in chemical and nuclear plants.<br>• Used in sputtering targets, cutting tools, and as a high-temperature material for applications requiring extreme durability. |
| **TIN** | One of Africa's large Tin producers. A trusted and competitive partner in sustainable supply chains, ranking among the world's top producers and home to a world-class tin smelting facility. | • Electronics, Tinplate, Pewter.<br>• Tin is also essential in the glass industry for making flat glass, in solar panels, and in various chemical applications as a stabilizer and catalyst. |
| **LITHIUM** | Emerging global powerhouse for quality lithium. | Powering rechargeable batteries for electronics and electric vehicles, improving the strength and lightness of alloys for aerospace and transportation, increasing the thermal and corrosion resistance of glass and ceramics, and acting as a medication for treating bipolar disorder. |
