# Traceability
& Compliance

*   **Regional Certification Mechanism (RCM):** Rwanda is among the most advanced implementers of the RCM, tracking the chain of custody of 3T minerals (Tin, Tungsten, Tantalum).
*   **Institutional Oversight:** RMB has established fully operational offices dedicated to mineral traceability and certification. These conduct regular mine inspections and produce jointly-validated reports with ITSCl, reinforcing Rwanda's credibility.
*   **Certification Partners:** Rwanda works with ITSCl and RCS Global to deliver independent, internationally recognized mineral traceability services.
*   **Innovation:** As mining services increasingly digitalize, Rwanda is pioneering the use of blockchain traceability – with LuNa Smelter as an early adopter of chain-of-custody blockchain technology.

# Geoscience
Laboratory

*   To complement its focus on traceability and value addition, Rwanda has invested in a **state-of-the-art Geoscience Laboratory** located in Nyarugunga, Kicukiro District. This facility provides high-end mineral testing, inspection, and certification services, and is currently undergoing international accreditation to match global standards.

# Core Goals
& Services

*   Serve as a **regional laboratory** for mineral testing, inspection, and certification across exploration, exploitation, and trading.
*   Provide tailored solutions for **mineral exploration, export certification, and environmental monitoring.**
*   Support export processes by ensuring reliable testing of samples for **revenue traceability.**
*   Deliver detailed analysis to **identify exact mineral types and concentrations, enabling better investment decisions, exploitation strategies, and taxation policies.**

The Geoscience Laboratory strengthens Rwanda's capacity to become not just a producer but also a center of excellence in mineral research, testing, and certification for the entire region.
