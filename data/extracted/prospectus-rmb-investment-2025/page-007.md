# A History of Growth

Mining operations formally began in the early 1930s, marking the start of a sector that has steadily evolved into one of Rwanda's leading industries. Through a series of progressive reforms, the sector has transformed into a cornerstone of the national economy.
By 2024, Rwanda's mining exports reached USD 1.75 billion, a dramatic increase from USD 373.4 million in 2017- a nearly fivefold growth in just seven years.

Looking forward, Rwanda's century-long history of mining success provides a solid foundation for the next phase of growth. With untapped reserves of lithium, rare earth elements, and high-value gemstones, combined with a government focus on value addition, traceability, and technology-driven exploration, Rwanda is well-positioned to emerge as a global hub for responsible, future-focused mineral investment.
