Rwanda's mining sector operates under a clear and modern legal framework designed to create certainty and confidence for investors. The law on mining and quarry operations establishes **Three categories of mining regimes**, each tailored to the scale and capacity of operators:

1
Small-Scale Mining
up to 50 hectares

2
Medium-Scale Mining
up to 100 hectares

3
Large-Scale Mining
up to 400 hectares

To facilitate the full mineral value chain,
five types of licenses are available:

*   Exploration
*   Mining
*   Processing
*   Trading
*   Service Provision

This licensing framework ensures that both local and international investors have access to opportunities across exploration, extraction, processing, and commercialization, with clear compliance requirements to protect investments and maintain global best practices.
