# Value Addition

Currently, a portion of Rwanda's mineral ores are exported as raw concentrates rather than fully processed metals. This presents a major investment opportunity in establishing processing plants for:

*   Tungsten refining
*   Gemstone cutting and polishing
*   Lithium processing
*   Beryl beneficiation
*   Service Provision
*   Trading

By investing in these opportunities, you will help boost export revenues, solidify Rwanda's role as a regional beneficiation hub, and secure strong, sustainable returns on your investment.
