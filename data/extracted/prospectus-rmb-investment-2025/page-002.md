# Executive Foreword

O n behalf of the Rwanda Mines, Petroleum and Gas Board (RMB), I am honored to welcome you to explore Rwanda's mining sector – a cornerstone of our nation's economic transformation and sustainable development.

Rwanda has earned a strong reputation as a reliable source of responsibly mined and fully traceable minerals. We are proud to host the world's highest-quality tantalum, stand as Africa's largest tungsten producer, and hold one of the most promising prospects for high-grade lithium globally. Building on these strengths, our vision is to advance mining excellence, accelerate mineral value addition, and position Rwanda as a regional hub for processing, beneficiation, innovation, and green mining practices.

**Alice Uwase**
Chief Executive Officer
Rwanda Mines, Petroleum and Gas Board (RMB)

Our diverse mineral portfolio is supported by an enabling environment grounded in political stability, transparency, and a business-friendly ecosystem. We are confident that investors and partners will find Rwanda to be a safe, strategic, and forward-looking destination where mining excellence and sustainability go hand in hand.

I also extend a special invitation to investors interested in upstream petroleum. The Lake Kivu basin represents not only an exciting exploration frontier, but also a gateway to sustainable partnerships and shared prosperity.
