# Hydrocarbons – Lake Kivu Rift Basin

Beyond minerals, Rwanda offers **frontier hydrocarbon opportunities** in the Lake Kivu Rift Basin, part of the prolific East African Rift System. Its geological features are analogous to proven petroleum basins in Uganda (Lake Albert), Kenya (Lokichar), and Sudan (Muglad), where significant discoveries have been made.

## Why Lake Kivu Basin?

*   **Strategic Geology:** Sedimentary thickness of 2.5–5.0 km confirmed through geophysical surveys.
*   **Exploration Database:** Over 460 km of seismic lines, marine gravity/magnetic studies, and geochemical analyses available.
*   **Favourable Petroleum System:** Presence of structural traps, thermogenic hydrocarbons, and organic-rich source rocks within the oil window.
*   **Infrastructure Advantage:** The East African Crude Oil Pipeline (EACOP), under construction, will significantly enhance the basin's commercial viability.
