# Minerals Global standing Key uses

## TUNGSTEN

Africa's largest producer

- Tungsten Carbide (Hardmetal):
  The largest use is in the form of tungsten carbide, which is a highly durable and wear-resistant material used for:
  - Cutting and Drilling: For cutting tools, drills, and mining equipment.
  - Construction: In heavy-duty tools for the construction industry.

- High-Temperature Applications:
  Tungsten's high melting point makes it ideal for:
  - Lighting: As filaments in light bulbs.
  - Welding: In welding electrodes.
  - Furnace Components: In various high-temperature industrial processes.

- Electronics and Electrical Equipment:
  Used for its electrical properties and ability to withstand heat in:
  - Electrical Contacts: For robust electrical connections.
  - Semiconductors: In electronic components.

- Aerospace and Defense:
  Valued for its density and strength in:
  - Armor and Ballistics: In military projectiles.
  - Aerospace Components: For counterweights and other high-stress parts.

- Radiation Shielding:
  Its high density makes it effective for shielding against radiation in:
  - Medical Devices: For radiation shielding in medical applications.
  - X-ray Tubes: As a target material in X-ray tubes.

- Specialty Applications:
  - Automotive: In engine parts and turbochargers.
  - Medical: Used in some surgical tools and implants.
  - Jewellery: In scratch-resistant rings and watches due to its hardness.
