# Rwanda at a Glance

**Geological Advantage**
Located in the Kibaran Belt spanning DRC, Rwanda, Burundi, Uganda & Tanzania – Rwanda hosts deposits of:

*   Tin, Tungsten, Tantalum (3Ts)
*   Lithium, Niobium, Rare Earths
*   Beryl, Phosphate, Gold, Gemstones
*   Industrial minerals (Granite, Clay, Sand)
