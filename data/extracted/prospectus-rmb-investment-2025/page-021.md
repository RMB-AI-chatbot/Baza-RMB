# Industrial Mining Anchored on Technology

While artisanal mining still exists in Rwanda, the sector is undergoing rapid reforms toward **professionalization and industrialization**. The demand for **modern mining equipment** - including drill rigs, bulldozers, and gravity table shakers - is growing as operators upgrade to semi-mechanized and eventually fully industrial operations.

At the same time, the sector is embracing smart mining technologies, including:

*   **Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Internet of Things (IoT)** applications for predictive operations.
*   **Drones and geospatial mapping** to improve exploration efficiency.
*   **Data-driven mineral resource management** for sustainable growth.
