# GASABO GOLD REFINERY
Located at the Masoro Industrial Zone

*   **Capacity:** 8 tons per month (480 kg refined within 30 hours).
*   **Utilization:** Currently operating at 70% of installed capacity.
*   **Output:** Produces fully refined gold with 99.999% purity, placing Rwanda among the few nations globally capable of this level of refinement.
*   **Strategic Impact:** The refinery provides full value addition to gold exports, eliminating the need for external processing and ensuring Rwanda captures more value within its economy.

# LUNA SMELTER
Located in Karuruma, Kigali

*   **Capacity:** 320 tons per month per Electric Arc Furnace (EAF).
*   **Current Output:** 200-220 tons per month, at approximately 68% utilization.
*   **Technology:** Uses cutting-edge EAF technology for cassiterite smelting.
*   **Significance:** The smelter contributes 26% of Rwanda's national tin export value, making it a cornerstone of the country's mineral beneficiation strategy.
