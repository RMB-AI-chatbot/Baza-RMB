## Exploration

The Government of Rwanda has invested heavily in
generating high-quality geological data across
newly identified Prospective Target Areas (PTAs).
These PTAs offer ready entry points for exploration
companies seeking to delineate and quantify
mineral resources. With modern data available,
investors can move with confidence and speed from
exploration to feasibility and production.
