gold refinery.

## GEMSTONES
Ranking among the largest
exporters of precious stones,
Rwanda is source of precious stones
such as amethyst, sapphire and
tourmaline.

Jewellery and decorative arts, industrial and
technological applications, and symbolic and
spiritual uses.

## INDUSTRIAL
MINERALS
Underexploited reserves of varieties
of industrial minerals

Essential, non-metallic resources fundamental to
many sectors, including construction (cement,
concrete, drywall), manufacturing (glass, ceramics,
plastics, paper, paints), agriculture (fertilizers), and
technology (electronics, solar panels).

## BERYLLIUM
Emerging global powerhouse for
beryllium

Extensively used in aerospace, defence, nuclear

properties.
